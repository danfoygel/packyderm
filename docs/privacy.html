Privacy Policy

Packyderm for iPhone · Effective November 21, 2026

Packyderm builds packing lists from your calendar and the weather. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer does not collect, store or receive any information about you.

Information that stays on your iPhone

  • Your calendar. If you allow it, Packyderm reads events in the calendars you choose to find upcoming trips. Events are processed on your device and are never uploaded or shared. Packyderm never changes your calendar.
  • Your location. If you tap "Use current location" in Settings, Packyderm reads your location once to set your home city. Only the city and its coordinates are saved, on your device.
  • Your voice. Read-aloud mode listens for "yes" or "no" while it reads your list. Speech is recognized on your device when your iPhone supports it. Audio is not recorded or saved.
  • Your trips and packing lists are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Services the app talks to

To work, Packyderm connects directly from your iPhone to services run by Apple. The developer has no server in between and does not see these requests. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

  • Apple Weather (WeatherKit). The locations and dates of your trip stops are sent to Apple to get forecasts and historical weather.
  • Apple Maps. Place searches, and your location if you choose "Use current location", are sent to Apple to find places and city names.
  • Apple Speech Recognition. If your iPhone can't recognize speech on the device, Read-aloud audio is sent to Apple to recognize "yes" or "no".

What the developer does not do

  • Collect personal information, calendar data, location history or usage data
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Sell or share any data

Children

Packyderm does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
