Packyderm

Packing lists built from your calendar and the weather.

Tap New Trip and Packyderm finds the trip on your calendar from flights, hotels and events away from home. It checks the weather for every stop and builds a packing list that adds rain gear, warm layers or sunscreen when you'll need them. Read-aloud mode reads the list while you pack, and you answer "yes" or "no".

Common questions

Packyderm didn't find my trip. Make sure your home city is set (Settings → Home) and the right calendars are chosen (Settings → Calendars). An event counts as travel if its location is more than about 50 miles from home, or if its title looks like "Flight to Denver", "Hotel …" or "Trip to Paris". You can always tap Enter trip manually.

Someone else's trip shows up. Turn off shared calendars (family, coworkers, holidays) in Settings → Calendars.

The weather says "typical". The forecast covers the next 10 days. For days further out, Packyderm shows the average for those dates over the past three years. Tap Refresh weather closer to the trip.

Read-aloud isn't hearing me. Allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings → Privacy & Security. The Yes and No buttons always work.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
